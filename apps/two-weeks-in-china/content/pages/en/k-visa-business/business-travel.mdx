## Definition & Scenarios
- **Definition**: Issued to personnel visiting China for commercial trade activities.
- **Scenarios**: Business inspections, client visits, contract signing, attending trade fairs, or business competitions.

## Core Documents: Invitation Letter
- **Invitee Info**: Name, gender, DOB, passport number, etc.
- **Visit Info**: Purpose, arrival/departure dates, places to visit, relationship with inviter, source of funds.
- **Inviter Info**: Name, address, phone, Official Seal, signature of legal rep.
- **PU/TE Letter**: Some consulates (e.g., Los Angeles) may require a government-issued Invitation Letter (PU/TE) with a barcode. Others may not. Check specific consular requirements.

## Digital Application Process (COVA)
- **Online Form**: Fill out the application online at consular.mfa.gov.cn/VISA/.
- **Walk-in Submission**: Most consulates (US, UK) have abolished appointments for visa submission once the status is 'Passport Submission Pending'.
- **Fingerprint Waiver**: Until Dec 31, 2026, fingerprints are waived for single/double entry M visas under 180 days.
