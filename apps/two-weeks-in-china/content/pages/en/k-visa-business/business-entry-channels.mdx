## Unilateral Visa-Free
- **Eligibility**: Citizens of France, Germany, Italy, Netherlands, Spain, Australia, NZ, etc. (48 countries).
- **Rights**: Visa-free entry for business, tourism, etc., for up to 30 days.
- **Validity**: Policy extended until Dec 31, 2026.

## 240-hour (10-day) Transit Visa-Free
- **Scenario**: Short business meetings while en route to a third country.
- **Rules**: Citizens of 55 countries (US, CA, UK, etc.) with a connecting ticket to a third country.
- **Scope**: Allowed to conduct business meetings, visit factories, or attend fairs in 24 provinces during the 10-day stay.

## Port Visa (VOA)
- **Target**: Urgent business matters (signing contracts, emergency repairs) with no time for embassy application.
- **Condition**: Requires an invitation letter explaining the urgency from an authorized Chinese entity. Apply upon arrival at ports like PVG or PEK.

## APEC Business Travel Card
Holders of valid APEC Business Travel Cards can enter visa-free.
