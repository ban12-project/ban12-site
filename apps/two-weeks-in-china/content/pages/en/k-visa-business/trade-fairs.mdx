## Exhibitions & Convenience
- **Typical Fairs**: HOMETEX SHENZHEN (Mar 2026), SinoPaper South (Apr 2026, Shenzhen).
- **Services**: Organizers often provide official invitation letters, business matching, and accommodation recommendations.

## Business Traveler Survival Guide
- **Digital Arrival Card**: From Nov 20, 2025, fill out online 24h before arrival to generate a QR code.
- **Payment**: Download Alipay or WeChat Pay and link international cards (Visa/Mastercard). High-end venues accept cards, but taxis/stores need apps.
- **Local Transport**: Use DiDi (via Alipay/WeChat mini-apps) for ride-hailing.
