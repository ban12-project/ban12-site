The Yangtze River Delta. China's most developed region with the densest high-speed rail network. Ideal for mixing business with leisure.

## Permitted Stay Area
Shanghai, Jiangsu Province, Zhejiang Province

## Key Entry Ports
- Shanghai: Pudong (PVG), Hongqiao (SHA), Port of Shanghai
- Jiangsu: Nanjing Lukou (NKG), Wuxi Shuofang (WUX)
- Zhejiang: Hangzhou Xiaoshan (HGH), Ningbo Lishe (NGB)

## Regional Rules & Tips
- High-Speed Hub: The best region for train travel. Shanghai -> Suzhou -> Hangzhou loop is fully legal.
- Open Jaw: You can enter via Shanghai and exit via Hangzhou to a third country.

## Recommended Itinerary
**Shanghai (3 days) + Suzhou (2 days) + Hangzhou (3 days) + Wuzhen/Nanjing (2 days)**
