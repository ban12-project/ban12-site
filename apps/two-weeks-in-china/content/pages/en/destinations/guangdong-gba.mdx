Province-wide visa-free access in Guangdong, plus seamless links to Hong Kong and Macau.

## Permitted Stay Area
Guangdong Province (All)

## Key Entry Ports
- Air: Guangzhou Baiyun (CAN), Shenzhen Bao'an (SZX), Jieyang Chaoshan (SWA)
- Land/Sea: Shenzhen Shekou Port, Nansha Port, HK-Zhuhai-Macau Bridge

## Regional Rules & Tips
- Hong Kong/Macau Factor: HK/Macau are 'Third Regions'. USA -> Guangzhou -> Hong Kong is a valid transit route.
- One Trip, Multiple Stops: Use HK as a springboard to explore Guangzhou, Shenzhen, and Foshan.
- Warning: Once you enter HK/Macau, you have left the mainland visa-free zone.

## Recommended Itinerary
**Guangzhou (Morning Tea) -> Shenzhen (Tech) -> Foshan (Kung Fu) -> Exit to HK**
