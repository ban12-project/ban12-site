The Beijing-Tianjin-Hebei integration area allows seamless travel across three administrative regions. Perfect for deep dives into Chinese history.

## Permitted Stay Area
Beijing, Tianjin, and Hebei Province

## Key Entry Ports
- Air: Beijing Capital (PEK), Beijing Daxing (PKX)
- Air: Tianjin Binhai (TSN), Shijiazhuang Zhengding (SJW)
- Sea/Rail: Tianjin Int'l Cruise Port, Qinhuangdao Port

## Regional Rules & Tips
- Seamless Travel: Move freely between Beijing, Tianjin, and Hebei (e.g., Beijing -> High-speed train to Tianjin -> Qinhuangdao).
- Duration: 240 Hours (10 Days) is enough for the Great Wall, Forbidden City, and Chengde Mountain Resort.
- Restriction: You cannot travel directly to Shanghai or Xi'an from here unless you have a visa or exit/re-enter.

## Recommended Itinerary
**Beijing (4 days) + Tianjin (2 days) + Chengde/Qinhuangdao (3 days)**
