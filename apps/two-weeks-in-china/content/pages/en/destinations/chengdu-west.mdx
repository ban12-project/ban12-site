The hub of Western China. Note that unlike the East, these regions are generally NOT interconnected for visa-free travel.

## Permitted Stay Area
Sichuan (Chengdu + 10 cities), Chongqing, Yunnan (Kunming + 8 cities)

## Key Entry Ports
- Chengdu Tianfu (TFU), Chengdu Shuangliu (CTU)
- Chongqing Jiangbei (CKG)
- Kunming Changshui (KMG)

## Regional Rules & Tips
- Not Interconnected: You typically cannot use one transit permit to travel between Chengdu and Kunming.
- Stay Local: Stick to the 11 administrative cities around Chengdu for the full 240 hours.
- Highlights: Pandas, Sanxingdui, Leshan Giant Buddha.

## Recommended Itinerary
**Chengdu (Pandas) -> Leshan (Buddha) -> Dujiangyan -> Sanxingdui**
