<InfoBox type="warning" title="The App Ecosystem">
Western apps often don't work or lack local data. You need the local equivalents to navigate, communicate, and travel.
</InfoBox>

## Navigation

### Apple Maps
Best for iOS users. Works without VPN and data is highly accurate.

### Amap (Gaode) / Baidu Maps
Most complete data (even public toilets), but interface is Chinese-only. Good if you can read some characters.

## Ride-Hailing

### DiDi (Greater China)
China's Uber. The 'Greater China' version has an English interface and in-app realtime chat translation. Crucial for avoiding scams.

## Translation

### DeepL / Google Translate
Download offline language packs before you arrive.

### Alipay
Use the built-in 'Scan' -> 'Translate' feature for restaurant menus.

## Entry

### Digital Arrival Card
New from Nov 2025. Fill out online to generate a QR code for entry, replacing paper forms.

<InfoBox type="success" title="Strategic Advice">
Don't book your entire trip, but DO book your first night's hotel. Save the hotel name/address in Chinese (screenshot) to show taxi drivers.
</InfoBox>
