<InfoBox type="warning" title="Paperless Travel">
China's High-Speed Rail (HSR) is the backbone of inter-city travel. The system is entirely paperless—your passport is your ticket.
</InfoBox>

## Trip.com
*Recommended for Travelers*

**Pros:**
- Friendly English interface.
- Supports international credit cards directly.
- 24/7 English customer support (crucial for changes/cancellations).

**Cons:**
- Charges a small service fee per ticket.

## 12306 (Official App)
*For Experts / Residents*

**Pros:**
- Official ticket source.
- No service fees.

**Cons:**
- Requires 'Identity Verification' which involves manual review.
- Process can take hours or verify fail for foreigners.
- Interface and support are less foreigner-friendly.

<InfoBox type="info" title="Boarding the Train">
Look for the 'Manual Lane' (Staff Lane) at the station gates. Show your passport to the staff or scan the photo page at supported automated gates.
</InfoBox>
