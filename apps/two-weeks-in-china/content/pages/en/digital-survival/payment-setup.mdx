<InfoBox type="warning" title="Core Context: A Cashless Society">
China has largely moved away from cash. Physical credit cards (Visa/Mastercard) are only accepted in high-end hotels and malls. Without mobile payment, daily life—from buying water to taking the subway—is nearly impossible.
</InfoBox>

## Setup Steps

### 1. Download & Register
Download the international version of Alipay or WeChat. Register using your overseas phone number.

### 2. Link International Cards
Link your Visa, Mastercard, or JCB card directly in the app's 'Wallet' section. (The complex 'TourCard' is no longer needed—direct binding works well now).

### 3. Verify Identity
Complete the 'Real-name Authentication' by uploading your passport photo. Do this BEFORE arrival to avoid network issues.

## Transaction Fees
- Under 200 CNY: Free
- Over 200 CNY: ~3% Service Fee

## Tips
- WeChat and Alipay are 'Super Apps'—use them for ride-hailing, food delivery, and metro tickets, not just paying.
- Always carry a small amount of cash (RMB) and a physical card as a backup.
