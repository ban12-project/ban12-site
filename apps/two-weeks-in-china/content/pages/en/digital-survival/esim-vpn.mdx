<InfoBox type="warning" title="The Core Challenge: The Great Firewall">
China's 'Great Firewall' blocks access to Google, WhatsApp, Instagram, YouTube, and many other Western services. Securing reliable internet access is your #1 survival priority upon arrival.
</InfoBox>

## eSIM (Highly Recommended)
The most stable and hassle-free solution for travelers.

**Advantages:**
- Data routed via HK/Singapore, bypassing the GFW automatically.
- No VPN configuration required.
- Works immediately upon landing.

**Recommended Providers:** Airalo, Nomad, Holafly, MobiMatter

> Set this up on your phone before you board your flight.

## VPN + Local SIM
Traditional method, but becoming less reliable.

**Drawbacks:**
- Requires purchasing a local Chinese SIM (China Unicom/Mobile).
- VPNs are actively blocked during sensitive periods.
- Connections can be unstable and drain battery life.

> Not recommended for short trips (e.g. 10-day visa-free transit).

<InfoBox type="warning" title="The Public Wi-Fi Trap">
Airport and public Wi-Fi networks usually require SMS Verification to log in. If you don't have a working roaming number or local SIM yet, you will be unable to connect, leading to 'digital isolation' immediately after landing.
</InfoBox>
